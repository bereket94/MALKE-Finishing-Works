WHO WE ARE

Creating Spaces That Inspire Living.

MALKE Finishing Works is a construction finishing and renovation business based in Addis Ababa, Ethiopia. We focus on delivering quality finishing solutions for residential and commercial spaces.

From interior and exterior finishing to painting, tiling, gypsum work and renovation, our goal is to make every project look its best.
